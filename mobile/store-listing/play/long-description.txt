**PriceScout helps thrift-store crews price donated items fast and confidently.**

Open the scanner, snap an item, and get a defensible suggested tag price in seconds.
PriceScout is built for thrift stores, estate-sale teams, and yard-sale operators who need speed and consistency at the sorting table.

**What you get**
- Fast camera-based item scans on Android
- Suggested tag price backed by real sold-comparable data
- Confidence signals like comp sample size and median
- Shared crew workflow so multiple staff can price in parallel
- Pricing tiers that fit weekend events or year-round stores

**Built for real floor operations**
PriceScout is designed for teams who sort donations and inventory in batches.
Instead of guessing or searching manually, your team snaps and gets a clear pricing recommendation that keeps tags consistent across shifts.

**From scan to sale-ready**
1. Open the scanner
2. Snap the item
3. Review comps and suggested tag price
4. Save the result for team visibility
5. Move to the next item

**Who uses PriceScout**
- Thrift store staff and managers
- Estate-sale crews
- Yard-sale operators
- Multi-table resale teams that need consistent pricing decisions

PriceScout keeps your pricing process quick, repeatable, and easier to train across the whole crew.
